# Accessibility

## Our commitment

The General Services Administration (GSA) is committed to equal access to Section508.gov for people with disabilities. The [Section508.gov accessibility policy](https://www.section508.gov/website-policies/#accessibility-policy) describes our commitment to Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act, as amended, and comparable access to information for federal employees and the public.

The [GSA accessibility statement](https://www.gsa.gov/website-information/accessibility-statement) describes the agency's Section 508 standards and WCAG 2.1 Level AA commitment, along with ongoing improvements. This repository's development guidance targets WCAG 2.2 Levels A and AA, tracking supplemental requirements separately from Section 508 obligations. This page is not a conformance assessment of the website or its tools.

## Report an accessibility barrier

For difficulty accessing Section508.gov, including its Accessibility Requirements Tool (ART), email [section.508@gsa.gov](mailto:section.508@gsa.gov). Please include:

- The affected page or resource URL.
- The barrier you encountered and what you were trying to do.
- Your contact information and preferred format for receiving the information.
- If available, steps to reproduce the problem and your browser, operating system, and assistive technology.

The Section508.gov policy says the team will respond as soon as possible and try to supply the requested information.

If you have a GitHub account, you may also use the repository's [issue templates](https://github.com/GSA/Section508.gov/issues/new/choose) to report accessibility barriers, bugs, or content issues, suggest changes, or share general website feedback. Choose the [accessibility issue form](https://github.com/GSA/Section508.gov/issues/new?template=04-accessibility-issue.yml) to report an accessibility barrier. GitHub issues are public; omit personal or sensitive information. Email is available if you do not have a GitHub account, GitHub itself presents a barrier, or you need to share contact details privately.

For agency accessibility assistance, contact [cio508help@gsa.gov](mailto:cio508help@gsa.gov). Questions about GSA's implementation of Section 508 policy may be directed to its Section 508 program manager, Mohammad Islam, at [mohammad.islam@gsa.gov](mailto:mohammad.islam@gsa.gov).

## Formal complaints and accommodations

An administrative Section 508 complaint may be filed with GSA's Office of Civil Rights within 180 days of the alleged discrimination. Contact the office at [202-501-0767](tel:+12025010767), or use Federal Relay at [800-777-8339](tel:+18007778339). Written complaints may be sent to:

GSA Office of Civil Rights  
1800 F Street, NW, Room 2340  
Washington, DC 20405

See the [GSA accessibility statement's complaint and accommodation procedures](https://www.gsa.gov/website-information/accessibility-statement) for details, including reasonable accommodations for federal employees and job applicants and telecommunications relay services.

## Documents and external resources

Some resources may require a third-party viewer. The [GSA accessibility statement](https://www.gsa.gov/website-information/accessibility-statement) lists accessibility aids and file viewers. Report inaccessible materials using the contact above and request an accessible format.

Links to external tools and websites are provided for convenience. GSA does not endorse those products or guarantee their Section 508 compliance. Consult the [Section508.gov linking policy](https://www.section508.gov/website-policies/#linking-policy) for details.

## Contribute accessible changes

Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the repository's [accessibility instructions](AGENTS.md). Changes should preserve or improve keyboard access, visible focus, semantic structure, contrast, meaningful links, text alternatives, accessible forms, and media alternatives.

Use the [development instructions](.github/accessibility-si/instructions/accessibility-development.md) and [generate, test and remediate workflow](.github/accessibility-si/workflows/generate-test-remediate.md). Include rendered-content checks, relevant automated results, and remaining human keyboard, visual, and assistive-technology review needs in pull requests. Automated checks alone do not establish conformance.

## Maintenance

Repository maintainers should keep this page's contacts, reporting links, and contribution guidance aligned with the published GSA and Section508.gov policies. Report outdated details through the channels above.
