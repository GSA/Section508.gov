If you’re looking for a quick way to stay up to date with accessibility guidance, tools, and resources, [**What’s New on Section508.gov**]({{seite.baseurl}}/whats-new/) is a great place to start. You can access “What’s New” at any time from the top navigation bar on Section508.gov.

Behind the scenes, it works automatically to surface the most recent activity across the site—so you don’t have to go searching for it.

Here's how What's New works:

* This page shows when content pages and blog posts were created and last updated.
* Items that are new or updated **within the past 30 days** are highlighted in the list so they're easy to spot.
* The most **recently updated content** appears first, helping you quickly see what's most current.
* Our team may also **"pin" selected items** to the top to draw attention to important updates.
* If there haven't been many recent changes, the page still shows a small **set of the most recently updated items** so it remains useful.

For accessibility professionals, this means you can rely on one page to:

* Catch newly published resources
* Spot important updates to existing guidance
* Stay aligned with evolving best practices

Just as importantly, it reflects real changes happening across the site --- not a manually curated list --- so what you see is always current.

[**What’s New on Section508.gov**]({{seite.baseurl}}/whats-new/) is designed to help you stay informed more easily. Whether you check it regularly or occasionally, it helps ensure you don't miss updates that could impact your work.
