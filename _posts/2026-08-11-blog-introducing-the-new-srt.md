Introducing the New and Improved Solicitation Review Tool
=========================================================

We're excited to announce the new and improved [Solicitation Review Tool (SRT)](https://srt.app.cloud.gov), which  checks federal solicitations for required Section 508 accessibility language. Designed to support federal acquisition professionals, Section 508 Program Managers, accessibility specialists, and other stakeholders, SRT makes it faster and easier to add the relevant Section 508 requirements for the information and communication technology (ICT) solution being procured.

SRT makes it easier than ever to evaluate both draft and published federal solicitations. 

-   For **draft solicitations**, SRT generates solicitation-ready Section 508 language tailored to the ICT being procured, making it faster to include the required accessibility language before publication.

-   For **published solicitations**, SRT analyzes the document and generates a detailed report identifying whether the required Section 508 solicitation language is present, helping users quickly spot missing or incomplete accessibility requirements.

By supporting both the creation and review of solicitations, SRT helps agencies improve the consistency and quality of Section 508 requirements throughout the acquisition process, making it easier to develop more accessible ICT procurements.

Review Solicitations with Confidence
------------------------------------

With expanded AI capabilities, SRT simplifies the process of identifying the appropriate Section 508 requirements based on the ICT being acquired.  SRT is available for use by federal government employees and contractors with a .gov or .mil email address. Email <srt@gsa.gov> with your request for access.

Users can:

-   Upload a draft solicitation to identify applicable Section 508 requirements before publication.

-   Upload a previously published solicitation to determine whether required Section 508 accessibility language is included.

-   Determine if a product being procured is ICT and therefore required to include Section 508 conformance as a requirement. 

-   Review all solicitations from your organization to view the rate of successful inclusion of Section 508 language.  

-   Receive recommendations for Section 508 solicitation language to include in your solicitation based on the ICT products and services being procured.

-   Generate reports identifying percentage conformance across your organization's published solicitations

-   Save time by reducing manual reviews and helping ensure accessibility requirements are consistently addressed.

How the SRT Supports Your Work
------------------------------

The Solicitation Review Tool supports a wide range of federal acquisition and accessibility responsibilities.

For example:

-   **Acquisition professionals** can identify the Section 508 requirements that apply to the ICT being procured and generate solicitation-ready language before publishing.

-   **Contracting officers** and contract specialists can review existing solicitations to verify that required Section 508 language has been included.

-   **Section 508 Program Managers** and accessibility specialists can evaluate their organization to understand how often Section 508 language is included on published solicitations.

-   **Program managers/Product managers** can better understand the accessibility requirements associated with the ICT products and solutions they are acquiring.

These capabilities help reduce the time spent reviewing solicitations while promoting more consistent application of Section 508 requirements across federal acquisitions.

Supporting Better Accessibility Outcomes
----------------------------------------

With AI assistance, SRT helps agencies improve the quality and consistency of Section 508 requirements throughout the procurement lifecycle. By identifying applicable accessibility requirements, generating recommended solicitation language, and reviewing existing solicitations for required Section 508 content, SRT helps agencies understand when and how Section 508 should be applied to a particular procurement.

Whether you're preparing a new solicitation, reviewing an existing one, supporting Section 508 compliance, or evaluating accessibility requirements during the acquisition process, SRT provides a practical resource to help streamline solicitation reviews and improve accessibility outcomes.

**Explore the [Solicitation Review Tool](https://srt.app.cloud.gov) and start reviewing federal solicitations for Section 508 accessibility requirements. Make accessibility a seamless part of your acquisition process by helping ensure your agency procures accessible ICT that meets Section 508 requirements.**
