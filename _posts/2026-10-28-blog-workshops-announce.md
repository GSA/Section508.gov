Federal agencies create and share thousands of documents every day. Making those documents accessible helps ensure people with disabilities have comparable access to government information and services.

Building an accessible workplace, however, takes more than a single accessibility specialist or accessibility remediation team. Everyone who creates documents has a role to play.

To help agencies build accessibility skills across their workforce, the Government-wide IT Accessibility Program is introducing a new collection of ready-to-use [Accessibility Training Workshop materials]({{site.baseurl}}/training/accessibility-training-workshops/) that agencies can use to conduct their own training sessions.

## Train your team to create accessible documents
The Accessibility Training Workshop materials are designed for individuals who want to help their teams learn how to create accessible documents.

The materials provide practical guidance and resources that you can use to conduct training within your organization. Use the materials to deliver a complete workshop, incorporate individual lessons into an existing training program, or revisit specific topics when your team needs a refresher.

The goal is to make document accessibility a skill that is shared across your team --- not knowledge that resides with only one or two accessibility specialists.

## Learn it. Practice it. Teach it.
The workshop materials focus on practical techniques that document authors can apply to their everyday work. Organizations can use these materials to teach staff how to identify and address common accessibility issues and apply accessible document creation techniques in their everyday work.

When more people know how to create accessible documents, accessibility can move upstream in the process --- from fixing problems after they are created to preventing many of those problems in the first place.

## Start training your team
You don't have to be an accessibility specialist to help your team get started. The Accessibility Training Workshop materials provide a structured, ready-to-use approach for teaching the fundamentals of document accessibility and sharing practical skills with the people who create documents every day.

Use the workshop materials to host a training session for your team, supplement an existing accessibility program, or help document authors develop skills they can apply immediately.

Explore the [Accessibility Training Workshops]({{site.baseurl}}/training/accessibility-training-workshops/) and start building accessibility skills across your team.
