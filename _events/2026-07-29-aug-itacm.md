The General Services Administration’s (GSA) Government-wide IT Accessibility Program will host an IT Accessibility Community Meeting on Tuesday, August 18, 2026, from 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM ET.

Join United States Postal Service accessibility leadership for a presentation and demo of their Section 508 automated software tool. Learn how their team is utilizing automation, intelligent agents and structured prompts to help teams build accessible software at scale.

**Presenters**  
**Agency Sponsor** - Nadereh Nasir - US Postal Service Section 508 Program Manager (USPS)  
**Robert Conaghan** - Test Engineer Senior, General Dynamics Information Technology  
**John Cotter** - Test Engineer Senior, General Dynamics Information Technology  
**Denise Koschmann** - Human Factors Engineer, Sr Principal  
**Tyler Maddox** - Test Engineer Senior, General Dynamics Information Technology  

## Event Details
**DATE:** Tuesday, August 18, 2026  
**TIME:** 1:00 PM ET  
**WHERE:** Virtual  
**WHO:** Section 508 Program Managers, Developers, Project Managers, Chief Information Officers, Chief Artificial Intelligence Officers, IT Accessibility implementors
