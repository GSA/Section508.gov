The General Services Administration’s (GSA) Government-wide IT Accessibility Program will host an IT Accessibility Community Meeting on Tuesday, October 13, 2026, from 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM ET.  

Join Cindy Schaffner, Senior Accessibility Specialist, and Hope Turner, USCIS Design System Program Manager, to learn how designers can prevent costly accessibility rework through thoughtful design.  

Cindy presents real-world Section 508 compliance problems — including recurring issues that appear across multiple projects — found in audits, while Hope demonstrates how designers could have prevented them early in the design phase when changes are quick and inexpensive.  

Discover how collaborating with developers, and accessibility testers, during design can shift accessibility left, reducing expensive post-development remediation and errors. Designers have more power than they realize to create compliant products from the start and share the responsibility of accessibility compliance.  

**Presenters**    
**Cindy Schaffner** - Senior Accessibility Specialist    
**Hope Turner** - USCIS Design System Program Manager
