The General Services Administration’s (GSA) Government-wide IT Accessibility Program will host an IT Accessibility Community Meeting (ITACM) on Tuesday, December 8, 2026, from 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM ET.

Join us for two complimentary presentations at December’s IT Accessibility Community Meeting (ITACM).

Angela Watkins, Section 508 Program Manager at the Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation (PBGC), will walk through “Market Research with Accessibility in Mind”, a step-by-step process for integrating digital accessibility into this stage of the acquisition lifecycle.

The presentation will discuss leveraging Super Intelligence (SI) prompts and other resources (Google, vendor websites, and email) to get vendor’s Accessibility Conformance Reporting (ACR), and using GSA’s Accessibility Requirements Tools (ART) to identify the evaluation criteria for the type of ICT being sought to prepare effective procurement packages.

Laura Miller, GSA Senior Accessibility Specialist will present an overview and short demonstration of the newly updated Solicitation Review Tool (SRT), which checks federal solicitations for required Section 508 accessibility language. 

Designed for federal acquisition professionals, Section 508 program managers, accessibility specialists, and other stakeholders, SRT helps users quickly identify and add relevant Section 508 requirements based on the information and communication technology (ICT) being procured.  

**Presenters**    
**Angela Watkins** - Section 508 Program Manager, Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation (PBGC)    
**Laura Miller** - Senior Accessibility Specialist, General Services Administration (GSA)
