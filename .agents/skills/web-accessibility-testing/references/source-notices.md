# Source attribution

The ICT Testing Baseline Portfolio is maintained by the U.S. Access Board and GSA with federal accessibility community contributors. Baseline identifiers, titles and relationships in this collection derive from [the published Portfolio](https://ictbaseline.access-board.gov/) and [its source repository](https://github.com/atbcb/ICTTestingBaseline). The original information source is the Federal Chief Information Officers Council Accessibility Community of Practice (ACOP); see [the upstream information-usage license](https://github.com/atbcb/ICTTestingBaseline/blob/main/LICENSE). The distribution revision and local file hashes are recorded in [the inventory](../../inventory.json). That manifest does not identify pinned revisions of the external standards or Baseline procedures. Record the official source versions and access dates used for an assessment; an upstream provenance catalog and case suite are not bundled here.

The local checklists, format guidance, workflows and supplemental mappings are adaptations authored for this collection. They must not be attributed to ACOP as original Baseline language or presented as replacements for the official procedures.

WCAG and WCAG2ICT are W3C publications and retain their respective document-use terms. This collection links to their normative/informative sources and supplies original action summaries; it does not republish their complete standards text. The repository's own license continues to apply to its original work.
