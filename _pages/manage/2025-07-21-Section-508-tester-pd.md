Some duties and responsibilities reflected in this content were informed by an interagency working group convened in response to Immediate Governmentwide Actions in <a href="https://bidenwhitehouse.archives.gov/omb/management/ofcio/m-24-08-strengthening-digital-accessibility-and-the-management-of-section-508-of-the-rehabilitation-act/#anchorIGWA" target="_blank" class="usa-link usa-link--external">OMB Memorandum M-24-08</a>. The working group brought together federal employees from multiple agencies to collect and analyze position descriptions and identify common accessibility-related competencies for Section 508 Program Managers and digital accessibility specialists. This content expands on the working group efforts.


## How to Use this Content


This resource provides a practical reference for federal agencies working to define, strengthen, or formalize accessibility-related roles and responsibilities across their workforce. The content is not prescriptive. Treat it as a starting point and adapt it to fit your agency's structure, existing position descriptions, and governance model.


For each role, you will find:


* **Key duties and responsibilities** drawn from OMB M-24-08 requirements and federal best practices. 
* **Sample position description language** you can incorporate into new or existing position descriptions. 
* **Alliance building and engagement strategies** to help role-holders collaborate effectively across the enterprise. 
* **Sample performance metrics** to help agencies measure and report on accessibility outcomes for each role. 
* **RACI charts** showing how each role intersects with other stakeholders on key accessibility activities.


## How to Apply This Content


**For HR professionals and hiring managers:** Use the sample position description language as a starting point. Incorporate the duties and responsibilities that reflect your agency's mission, organizational structure, and existing classification standards. You do not need to include every item; select what is relevant and tailor the language to align with your agency's approved position descriptions and Office of Personnel Management (OPM) classification guidance.


**For Section 508 Program Managers:** Use the best practices, alliance building strategies, and RACI charts to identify gaps in your agency's current accessibility governance, clarify role boundaries, and build the cross-functional relationships needed to sustain an effective program.


**For agency leadership and executives:** Use the metrics and engagement strategies to establish accountability, set expectations with key partners, and integrate accessibility into existing governance and performance management structures.


**For IT Project Managers and Contracting Officers:** Use the duties, responsibilities, and RACI charts to understand your role in the broader accessibility ecosystem and identify where coordination with other stakeholders is required.


## A Few Important Notes


* **This content does not replace OPM classification standards.** All position description language must align with applicable OPM classification guidance and your agency's HR policies before use. 
* **Tailor, do not copy wholesale.** Every agency has a different structure. Take what is relevant, leave what is not, and adjust the language to reflect how your agency actually operates. 
* **Use the RACI charts as a conversation starter.** The charts reflect recommended role boundaries but your agency may assign responsibilities differently. Use them to facilitate conversations about ownership, coordination, and accountability. 
* **Metrics are a starting point.** The sample metrics reflect common measures of accessibility performance. Select the metrics that align with your agency's existing reporting structures and data collection capabilities.

 <h3>Key Section 508-related Responsibilities</h3>
   <div class="grid-col-12 summary-box--icon-requirement">
  <ul class="usa-icon-list padding-right-2 force-max-width">
      <li class="usa-icon-list__item">
          <div class="usa-icon-list__icon"><svg class="usa-icon icon-size" aria-hidden="true" role="img"><use xlink:href="{{site.baseurl}}/assets/img/sprite.svg#format_quote"></use></svg></div>
          <div class="usa-icon-list__content force-max-width">
              <div class="box-heading"><h4 style="margin-top:0; margin-bottom:0.5rem;">Requirements from <a href="https://bidenwhitehouse.archives.gov/omb/management/ofcio/m-24-08-strengthening-digital-accessibility-and-the-management-of-section-508-of-the-rehabilitation-act/" target="_blank" class="usa-link usa-link--external">OMB’s M-24-08</a></h4><hr></div>
              <div class="box-text">
   <ul>
   <li>Reviews and complies with FAR section 11.002(f) and subpart 39.2 by ensuring Section 508 standards are considered in acquisition planning documents and procurement requirements for ICT and that deliverables meet accessibility standards.</li>
   <li>Includes applicable Section 508 accessibility standards in requirements and acquisition planning documents and agreement, per FAR section 7.105(b)(5)(iv).</li>
   </ul>
   </div>
   </div>
          </li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="grid-col-12 summary-box--icon-requirement">
  <ul class="usa-icon-list padding-right-2 force-max-width">
      <li class="usa-icon-list__item">
          <div class="usa-icon-list__icon"><svg class="usa-icon icon-size" aria-hidden="true" role="img"><use xlink:href="{{site.baseurl}}/assets/img/sprite.svg#format_star"></use></svg></div>
          <div class="usa-icon-list__content force-max-width">
              <div class="box-heading"><h4 style="margin-top:0; margin-bottom:0.5rem;">Best Practices</h4><hr></div>
              <div class="box-text">
<ul>
 <li>Coordinates with program offices, the Section 508 Program Manager, and technical staff to determine applicable accessibility requirements and identify any exceptions.</li>
 <li>Ensures solicitations clearly define accessibility requirements, deliverables, and acceptance criteria, including requirements for Accessibility Conformance Reports (ACRs) or equivalent documentation.</li>
 <li>Evaluates vendor-submitted accessibility documentation such as ACRs or test reports as part of the acquisition process, in coordination with subject matter experts.</li>
 <li>Ensures accessibility considerations are included in source selection criteria and technical evaluations, as appropriate, and documented in evaluation records.</li>
 <li>Incorporates accessibility requirements into contract terms and conditions, including provisions for testing, validation, and remediation of nonconforming ICT.</li>
 <li>Documents accessibility decisions, including applicability, exceptions, and evaluation outcomes, in the official contract file.</li>
 <li>Monitors contractor compliance with accessibility requirements throughout contract performance, in coordination with the Contracting Officer’s Representative (COR).</li>
 <li>Enforces contractual accessibility requirements, including requiring remediation of defects, withholding acceptance when requirements are not met, and taking corrective action as appropriate.</li>
 <li>Ensures accessibility requirements are addressed in contract modifications, renewals, and option exercises.</li>
</ul>
</div>
   </div>
          </li>
  </ul>
</div>


<div class="grid-col-12 summary-box--icon-suggested">

              <div class="box-heading"><h3 style="margin-top:0; margin-bottom:0.5rem;">Sample Text to Include in a Position Description</h3><hr></div>
              <div class="box-text">
<ul>
<li>Is responsible for ensuring that acquisitions of information and communication technology (ICT) comply with applicable accessibility requirements. The CO incorporates Section 508 standards into acquisition planning, solicitations, and contracts, and ensures that accessibility requirements are clearly defined and enforceable.</li>
<li>Collaborates with program offices, the Chief Information Officer (CIO), and the Section 508 program manager to determine applicable accessibility requirements and to evaluate vendor-submitted accessibility documentation.</li>
<li>Ensures that accessibility considerations are included in source selection and that all decisions related to accessibility are appropriately documented. The CO administers contracts to ensure contractor compliance with accessibility requirements and takes appropriate action when deliverables do not meet established standards.</li>
</ul>
</div>
 </div>
      </li>
  </ul>
</div>

              <div class="box-text">
<h4>Program Office Partnership</h4>
<ul>
 <li>
   Require requirements packages that include:
   <ul>
     <li>Sufficient accessibility requirements.</li>
     <li>Acceptance criteria tied to testing.</li>
   </ul>
 </li>
 <li>Push back on incomplete submissions.</li>
</ul>


<h4>Section 508 Program Manager Collaboration</h4>


<ul>
 <li>
   Use subject matter experts to:
   <ul>
     <li>Interpret ACRs and review test results.</li>
     <li>Identify accessibility risk in vendor claims.</li>
   </ul>
 </li>
 <li>Establish a standard review workflow.</li>
</ul>


<h4>CIO and Technical Team Coordination</h4>


<ul>
 <li>
   Align on:
   <ul>
     <li>Accessibility risk acceptance.</li>
     <li>When independent testing is required pre-award.</li>
   </ul>
 </li>
</ul>


<h4>COR Relationship</h4>


<ul>
 <li>
   Ensure contracting officer representatives (COR):
   <ul>
     <li>Understand ICT accessibility requirements.</li>
     <li>Are prepared to validate deliverables and enforce requirements.</li>
   </ul>
 </li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Tip:</strong> Accessibility enforcement often fails here if expectations aren’t understood.</p>

<h4>Vendor Communication</h4>


<ul>
 <li>
   Set expectations early regarding how:
   <ul>
     <li>ICT accessibility will be evaluated.</li>
     <li>Documentation will be validated.</li>
     <li>Noncompliance will require remediation.</li>
   </ul>
 </li>
</ul>
</div>
 </div>
      </li>
  </ul>
</div>

              <div class="box-text">
   <h4>Pre-Award Compliance</h4>
<ul>
 <li>Percentage of ICT solicitations that include appropriate Section 508 requirements.</li>
 <li>Percentage of acquisitions that included accessibility criteria in contract documents during the selection or technical evaluation process.</li>
</ul>


<h4>Evaluation Quality</h4>
<ul>
 <li>Percentage of awards supported by reviewed and validated ACRs or similar documentation.</li>
 <li>Reduction in awards with incomplete or insufficient accessibility documentation.</li>
</ul>


<h4>Documentation and File Integrity</h4>
<ul>
 <li>Percentage of contract files with complete accessibility documentation, such as requirements, evaluations, and exceptions.</li>
</ul>


<h4>Post-Award Enforcement</h4>
<ul>
 <li>Percentage of contracts where accessibility requirements are enforced at acceptance.</li>
 <li>Number of accessibility-related issues identified post-award due to insufficient pre-award evaluation or lack of accessibility criteria used in the evaluation process.</li>
</ul>


<h4>Risk Reduction</h4>
<ul>
 <li>Reduction in contract modifications related to accessibility gaps.</li>
 <li>Percentage of high-risk ICT procurements escalated for additional review.</li>
</ul>
 </div>
 </div>
      </li>
  </ul>

  <h2 class="usa-accordion__heading">
      <button id="contracting-officer-rep" type="button" class="usa-accordion__button" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="a2">Contracting Officer's Representative (COR)</button>
</h2>
  <div id="a2" class="usa-accordion__content">

<h3>Key Section 508-related Responsibilities</h3>
  <div class="grid-col-12 summary-box--icon-requirement">
 <ul class="usa-icon-list padding-right-2 force-max-width">
     <li class="usa-icon-list__item">
         <div class="usa-icon-list__icon"><svg class="usa-icon icon-size" aria-hidden="true" role="img"><use xlink:href="{{site.baseurl}}/assets/img/sprite.svg#format_quote"></use></svg></div>
         <div class="usa-icon-list__content force-max-width">
             <div class="box-heading"><h4 style="margin-top:0; margin-bottom:0.5rem;">Requirements from <a href="https://bidenwhitehouse.archives.gov/omb/management/ofcio/m-24-08-strengthening-digital-accessibility-and-the-management-of-section-508-of-the-rehabilitation-act/" target="_blank" class="usa-link usa-link--external">OMB’s M-24-08</a></h4><hr></div>
             <div class="box-text">
        <ul>
        <li>Includes applicable Section 508 accessibility standards in acquisition planning and requirement documents.</li>
        <li>Verifies that products or services delivered under an agreement meet the accessibility terms and conditions included in a contract prior to accepting deliverables.</li>
        </ul>
  </div>
  </div>
         </li>
 </ul>
</div>

<div class="grid-col-12 summary-box--icon-requirement">
 <ul class="usa-icon-list padding-right-2 force-max-width">
     <li class="usa-icon-list__item">
         <div class="usa-icon-list__icon"><svg class="usa-icon icon-size" aria-hidden="true" role="img"><use xlink:href="{{site.baseurl}}/assets/img/sprite.svg#format_star"></use></svg></div>
         <div class="usa-icon-list__content force-max-width">
             <div class="box-heading"><h4 style="margin-top:0; margin-bottom:0.5rem;">Best Practices</h4><hr></div>
             <div class="box-text">
            <ul>
  <li>Supports the development of acquisition requirements by ensuring applicable Section 508 standards are identified and included in requirements and acquisition planning documents.</li>
  <li>Collaborates with the contracting officer (CO), Section 508 program manager, and technical staff to define accessibility requirements, deliverables, and acceptance criteria.</li>
  <li>Reviews contractor deliverables to verify conformance with contractual accessibility requirements prior to acceptance.</li>
  <li>Coordinates accessibility testing and validation activities, including working with subject matter experts to assess conformance of ICT products and services.</li>
  <li>Documents accessibility evaluation results, including identified defects, and provides recommendations to the CO regarding acceptance or rejection of deliverables.</li>
  <li>Tracks and manages accessibility defects identified during testing, ensuring issues are communicated to the contractor and remediated within established timelines.</li>
  <li>Monitors contractor performance related to accessibility requirements throughout the period of performance and escalates issues to the CO as needed.</li>
  <li>Ensures accessibility requirements are maintained and enforced in contract modifications, task orders, and iterative deliverables such as agile releases.</li>
  <li>Validates that required accessibility documentation, such as Accessibility Conformance Reports, are delivered, current, and reflect the actual product being provided.</li>
  <li>Maintains complete and accurate records of ICT accessibility-related activities, including testing results, communications, and acceptance decisions.</li>
  <li>Coordinates with program offices, developers, and content owners to ensure accessibility is addressed throughout implementation — not just at final delivery.</li>
</ul>
</div>
  </div>
         </li>
 </ul>
</div>

The COR monitors contractor performance, tracks accessibility-related issues, and ensures that identified defects are remediated in accordance with contract requirements.</li>

             <div class="box-text"><h3 style="margin-top:0; margin-bottom:0.5rem;">Strategies For Engagement & Alliance Building</h3><hr></div>
             <div class="box-text">
<h4>CO Partnership</h4>
<ul>
  <li>Establish joint-accountability early on:
    <ul>
      <li>Accessibility risk determination.</li>
      <li>What documentation is required for ICT acceptance.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li>Keep the CO informed.</li>
</ul>

<h4>Section 508 Program Manager Collaboration</h4>
<ul>
  <li>Use SMEs for:
    <ul>
      <li>Test planning.</li>
      <li>Interpreting results.</li>
      <li>Risk-based decisions.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li>Avoid “self-certification” by vendors without validation.</li>
</ul>

<h4>Technical and Program Team Integration</h4>
<ul>
  <li>Work closely with:
    <ul>
      <li>Developers.</li>
      <li>Product owners.</li>
      <li>Content teams.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li>Ensure accessibility is tested iteratively, not just at final delivery.</li>
  <li>Monitor, track and reduce the number and severity of defects introduced during updates.</li>
</ul>

<h4>Vendor Relationship Management</h4>
<ul>

<h3>Key Section 508-related Responsibilities</h3>
  <div class="grid-col-12 summary-box--icon-requirement">
 <ul class="usa-icon-list padding-right-2 force-max-width">
     <li class="usa-icon-list__item">
         <div class="usa-icon-list__icon"><svg class="usa-icon icon-size" aria-hidden="true" role="img"><use xlink:href="{{site.baseurl}}/assets/img/sprite.svg#format_quote"></use></svg></div>
         <div class="usa-icon-list__content force-max-width">
             <div class="box-heading"><h4 style="margin-top:0; margin-bottom:0.5rem;">Requirements from <a href="https://bidenwhitehouse.archives.gov/omb/management/ofcio/m-24-08-strengthening-digital-accessibility-and-the-management-of-section-508-of-the-rehabilitation-act/" target="_blank" class="usa-link usa-link--external">OMB’s M-24-08</a></h4><hr></div>
             <div class="box-text">
<ul>
  <li>Manages the Section 508 Program on behalf of the agency CIO or designee, including developing and maintaining agency policies, guidebooks, and disseminating best practices.</li>
  <li>Supports the agency’s efforts to create an accessible and inclusive ICT environment for all employees and members of the public with disabilities.</li>
  <li>Coordinates and collaborates with other staff responsible for Rehabilitation Act compliance, including the provision of reasonable accommodations.</li>
  <li>Provides consultation to, or participates in, agency capital planning, business case development, and IT governance to ensure ICT activities appropriately integrate Section 508 compliance across the IT and acquisition lifecycles.</li>
  <li>Assesses and seeks to address the needs of the public and employees with disabilities with regard to ICT accessibility.</li>
  <li>Manages and coordinates:
    <ul>
      <li>Assistance to acquisition officials and developers in procuring or building accessible ICT.</li>
      <li>Evaluation of web content, software, and hardware for Section 508 conformance.</li>
      <li>Section 508 training.</li>
      <li>Creation and review of electronic documents for Section 508 conformance.</li>
      <li>Provision of alternative formats.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
</ul>
  </div>
  </div>
         </li>
 </ul>
</div>

<div class="grid-col-12 summary-box--icon-requirement">
 <ul class="usa-icon-list padding-right-2 force-max-width">
     <li class="usa-icon-list__item">

         <div class="usa-icon-list__content force-max-width">
             <div class="box-heading"><h4 style="margin-top:0; margin-bottom:0.5rem;">Best Practices</h4><hr></div>
             <div class="box-text">
<ul>
  <li>Provides Section 508 guidance to agency programs and project teams for buying, building, maintaining, and using technology products and services.</li>
  <li>Leads compliance management activities for digital services and technology covered under Section 508, including:
    <ul>
      <li>Addressing compliance requirements in design, acquisition, development, and maintenance lifecycles and processes.</li>
      <li>Ensuring conformance claims are validated.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li>Develops policy, supports budget formulation, and addresses issues associated with digital accessibility with agency subcomponents, if applicable.</li>
  <li>Promotes Section 508 awareness through training and outreach activities and advocates use of shared resources.</li>
  <li>Leads technical discussions, meetings, conferences, demonstrations, and negotiations to improve accessibility of agency IT products and solutions.</li>
  <li>Collaborates with internal and external stakeholders to promote accessibility and with customers or business partners.</li>
  <li>Analyzes and re-engineers business processes to improve efficacy and efficiency.</li>
  <li>Provides consultation and advice on development and implementation of digital accessibility plans, including development of remediation plans, risk analyses, and review of contractor accessibility provisions.</li>
  <li>Provides support for Section 508 testing and for remediating accessible electronic content, such as documents and forms, to conform to the Section 508 standards.</li>
  <li>Oversees the requirements planning, procurement, design, modification, integration, testing, and deployment of accessible ICT.</li>
  <li>Tests ICT, such as large or complex web applications or electronic content, for Section 508 requirements and accessibility using a regimented test methodology that includes automated web scanning and testing, manual code inspection, and user testing with assistive technology, and delivers design recommendations to improve the accessibility of ICT.</li>
  <li>Coordinates the collection of data to complete the annual Section 508 Assessment.</li>
  <li>Develops and implements agencywide policy that ensures Section 508 compliance and outlines validation measures that can be tracked and processed.</li>
  <li>Reviews and approves ICT products and services for conformance to the Section 508 standards.</li>
  <li>Reviews technology solicitations for proper inclusion of Section 508 requirements.</li>
  <li>Serves as a Section 508 subject matter expert (SME) for technical evaluation of solicitations.</li>
  <li>Explains technical and functional accessibility concepts to technical and nontechnical personnel verbally, in written communications, and through briefings and formal presentations.</li>
  <li>Encourages professional development and recognition of accessibility professionals across the agency.</li>
</ul>

<ul>

Develops and maintains agency Section 508 policies, procedures, guidebooks, standards, and best practices to support compliance with Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act and related accessibility requirements.</li>
  <li>Through oversight of ICT accessibility programs, ensures effective planning, management, validation, and continuous improvement of the accessibility of information and communication technology systems, digital services, electronic content, and related products used by employees and members of the public.</li>
  <li>Provides expert guidance to program offices, acquisition officials, project teams, developers, designers, testers, content creators, and agency leadership on integrating Section 508 requirements into acquisition, design, development, testing, deployment, maintenance, and governance activities.</li>
  <li>Coordinates agency-wide Section 508 training, testing, reporting, remediation planning, risk management, and annual assessment activities. Reviews accessibility documentation, supports validation of conformance claims, and advises on acceptance, remediation, and risk mitigation for ICT products and services.</li>

             <div class="box-text"><h3 style="margin-top:0; margin-bottom:0.5rem;">Strategies For Engagement & Alliance Building</h3><hr></div>
             <div class="box-text">
<h4>CIO and Agency Leadership</h4>
<ul>
  <li>Align the Section 508 Program with agency mission priorities, IT governance, customer experience, and digital service objectives.</li>
  <li>Provide regular briefings on program status, risks, resource needs, compliance trends, and annual assessment outcomes.</li>
  <li>Frame accessibility as a core requirement for mission delivery, public access, workforce inclusion, and risk management.</li>
  <li>Secure leadership support for policy enforcement, staffing, training, and remediation priorities.</li>
</ul>

<h4>Deputy Section 508 Program Manager</h4>
<ul>
  <li>Delegate operational responsibilities for program execution, coordination, metrics tracking, and stakeholder engagement.</li>
  <li>Align on priorities, escalation paths, staffing needs, and resource allocation.</li>
  <li>Coordinate policy implementation, training delivery, testing oversight, and remediation tracking.</li>
  <li>Ensure continuity of program operations and consistent communication across agency components.</li>
</ul>

<h4>Section 508 SMEs</h4>
<ul>
  <li>Rely on SMEs for technical and policy interpretation of complex accessibility requirements.</li>
  <li>Co-develop guidance, job aids, testing approaches, risk models, and training materials.</li>
  <li>Ensure consistent application of Section 508 Standards across acquisitions, development efforts, content, and testing.</li>
  <li>Use SME expertise to resolve complex or high-risk accessibility questions.</li>
</ul>

<h4>Acquisition Officials</h4>
<ul>
  <li>Ensure Section 508 requirements are included early in acquisition planning, market research, solicitations, contracts, task orders, and acceptance criteria.</li>
  <li>Support review of ACRs, vendor claims, and accessibility test reports.</li>
  <li>Promote consistent contract language, deliverable expectations, and validation practices.</li>
  <li>Help acquisition teams identify and mitigate accessibility risks before award and prior to acceptance.</li>
</ul>

<h4>IT Governance, Enterprise Architecture, and Capital Planning Teams</h4>
<ul>
  <li>Embed Section 508 checkpoints into IT governance, architecture review boards, investment reviews, and capital planning processes.</li>
  <li>Ensure accessibility is considered in technology roadmaps, enterprise standards, reference architectures, and system design decisions.</li>
  <li>Identify accessibility risks early in the lifecycle and require mitigation plans for high-impact systems.</li>
  <li>Promote accessible platforms, tools, templates, and reusable patterns across the enterprise.</li>
</ul>

<h4>IT PMs, Product Managers, Developers, and UX Teams</h4>
<ul>
  <li>Ensure Section 508 requirements are incorporated into user stories, acceptance criteria, product roadmaps, release planning, and development workflows.</li>
  <li>Promote iterative accessibility testing throughout the software development lifecycle (SDLC) or Agile process, rather than only at final delivery.</li>
  <li>Support remediation planning and prioritization for accessibility defects.</li>
  <li>Encourage teams to build accessibility into design and development practices from the outset.</li>
</ul>

<h4>Testers</h4>
<ul>
  <li>Coordinate approved testing methodologies, tools, reporting formats, and severity definitions.</li>
  <li>Use testing results to identify trends, recurring defects, and training needs.</li>
  <li>Ensure defects are tracked, remediated, and validated prior to release or acceptance.</li>
  <li>Support consistent testing practices across products, platforms, and content types.</li>
</ul>

<h4>Content Managers, Content Creators, and Communications Teams</h4>
<ul>
  <li>Establish accessible content standards, templates, workflows, and review processes.</li>
  <li>Promote accessible authoring practices for documents, web content, multimedia, presentations, social media, and internal communications.</li>
  <li>Ensure accessibility is included in publishing workflows and communications planning.</li>
  <li>Prioritize remediation of high-impact, high-visibility, or frequently accessed content.</li>
</ul>

<h4>Customer Experience and Reasonable Accommodation Staff</h4>
<ul>
  <li>Coordinate to identify barriers experienced by individuals with disabilities.</li>
  <li>Use customer feedback, accommodation trends, usability findings, and service data to improve ICT accessibility.</li>
  <li>Ensure accessibility improvements reduce the need for individual workarounds where systemic fixes are possible.</li>
</ul>

<h4>Program Offices and Business Owners</h4>
<ul>
  <li>Clarify shared responsibility for Section 508 implementation within programs and services.</li>
  <li>Support integration of accessibility into program planning, service delivery, budgeting, and performance management.</li>
  <li>Help program offices understand accessibility risks, obligations, and remediation priorities.</li>
  <li>Encourage ownership of accessibility outcomes beyond the Section 508 Program office.</li>
</ul>

 </ul>

  <h2 class="usa-accordion__heading">
      <button id="ux_ui" type="button" class="usa-accordion__button" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="a13">User Experience (UX) or User Interface (UI) Designer</button>
</h2>
  <div id="a13" class="usa-accordion__content">


<h3>Key Section 508-related Responsibilities</h3>

<div class="grid-col-12 summary-box--icon-requirement">
 <ul class="usa-icon-list padding-right-2 force-max-width">
     <li class="usa-icon-list__item">
         <div class="usa-icon-list__icon"><svg class="usa-icon icon-size" aria-hidden="true" role="img"><use xlink:href="{{site.baseurl}}/assets/img/sprite.svg#format_star"></use></svg></div>
         <div class="usa-icon-list__content force-max-width">
             <div class="box-heading"><h4 style="margin-top:0; margin-bottom:0.5rem;">Best Practices</h4><hr></div>
             <div class="box-text">
<ul>
  <li>Designs user interfaces and experiences for ICT that conform to Section 508 Standards and support usability for individuals with disabilities.</li>
  <li>Applies accessible design principles, including color contrast, typography, layout, spacing, and visual hierarchy.</li>
  <li>Ensures designs support keyboard navigation, logical focus order, and clear interaction patterns.</li>
  <li>Creates and maintains accessible design components, patterns, and style guides that align with agency design systems.</li>
  <li>Specifies accessibility requirements within design artifacts, such as wireframes, mockups, prototypes, and annotations, including form labels, error handling, and interaction behaviors.</li>
  <li>Collaborates with developers to ensure design intent is implemented accurately and accessibly.</li>
  <li>Works with the Section 508 program manager and SMEs to interpret ICT accessibility requirements and incorporate them into design solutions.</li>
  <li>Participates in usability and accessibility testing, including testing with assistive technologies and users with disabilities, as appropriate.</li>
  <li>Identifies and resolves potential accessibility barriers during the design phase.</li>
  <li>Ensures consistency of accessible design patterns across products, platforms, and services.</li>
  <li>Supports the remediation of accessibility issues by updating design solutions and guidance.</li>
  <li>Promotes inclusive design practices that go beyond compliance to improve overall user experience.</li>
</ul>
</div>
  </div>
         </li>
 </ul>
</div>


<div class="grid-col-12 summary-box--icon-suggested">
 <ul class="usa-icon-list padding-right-2 force-max-width">
     <li class="usa-icon-list__item">
         <div class="usa-icon-list__icon"><svg class="usa-icon icon-size" aria-hidden="true" role="img"><use xlink:href="{{site.baseurl}}/assets/img/sprite.svg#lightbulb_outline"></use></svg></div>
         <div class="usa-icon-list__content force-max-width">
             <div class="box-heading"><h3 style="margin-top:0; margin-bottom:0.5rem;">Sample Text to Include in a Position Description</h3><hr></div>
             <div class="box-text">
<ul>
  <li>Ensures that all designs comply with accessibility requirements under Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act and support usability for individuals with disabilities.</li>
  <li>Applies accessible design principles to layouts, interactions, and visual elements, and incorporates accessibility requirements into design artifacts and specifications.</li>
  <li>Collaborates with developers, product managers, and the Section 508 program manager to ensure accessibility is integrated into the design and development lifecycle.</li>
  <li>Participates in usability and accessibility testing and contributes to the development of accessible design systems and patterns.</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>
     </li>
 </ul>
